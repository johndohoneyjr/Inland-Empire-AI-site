THE FIRST WIN SHOULD FEEL REAL.

Think useful.
Not all-encompassing.

  • A reusable prompt workflow for first-draft customer communications.
  • A local model tested on an approved sample document.
  • An agent that organizes a selected folder, with changes reviewed first.
  • A Claude Code workflow for a small, reviewable development task.

These are possible starting points, not guaranteed deliverables. Your kickoff is scoped around your business.

WHAT “24 HOURS” MEANS

An ambitious start.
An honest scope.

The target is to get an agreed, focused workflow into your team’s hands within 24 hours of a scheduled kickoff, once prerequisites are ready.

It is not a promise that every integration or project can be completed in a day. Hardware delivery, missing licenses, access approvals, complex integrations, and data preparation can extend the timeline.

We’ll agree on scope, availability, timing, and costs before work begins. No hidden “instant transformation” claims.

GET KICKOFF-READY.

A little preparation. A better start.

What should we bring to the first conversation?

A task that takes more time than it should, the tools your team uses, and an idea of who will own the workflow. Don’t send passwords, sensitive customer information, or confidential documents with your initial inquiry.

Does a local agent require a powerful GPU?

Not always. Requirements depend on the model, task, and whether inference is local or cloud-based. We’ll assess your hardware before choosing a setup and explain performance trade-offs.

What happens after the kickoff?

You’ll have the agreed walkthrough and usage guidance for the scoped workflow. Any additional training, maintenance, or expanded integrations can be discussed separately; ongoing support is not automatically included.

Your first step doesn’t need to be big.
It just needs to be useful.
